# Decision request

## Decision required

One sentence describing the user-facing, privacy, scope, visual, cost, or irreversible choice.

## Why it is needed now

What part of the task is blocked, and what work can continue independently?

## Option A

Describe the experience, benefits, costs, and risks.

## Option B

Describe the experience, benefits, costs, and risks.

## Recommendation

Recommend one option using product evidence and explicit tradeoffs. Do not manufacture a preference when the options are genuinely balanced.

## Reversibility

Explain what would be difficult to change later and what data or user expectations each option creates.
